## Device ecosystem and process safety

Inventory models, firmware, protocols, owners and physical function before assessing weaknesses. Information gathering is distinct from vulnerability scanning, gaining access and changing controller logic. OT includes PLCs, SCADA and distributed control systems; controller feedback loops and plant-wide coordination describe different abstractions. Safety and process continuity constrain even technically permitted probes or isolation. Coordinate stop authority with the system owner.

Classic Modbus TCP uses TCP 502 and function-code exchanges for coils/registers, without built-in authentication/encryption in that traditional form. TCP 102 is ISO-TSAP and is used by S7 communications; it is not a universal proof of a Siemens model. A protocol label or open service port is not an exploit result. MQTT is a publish/subscribe messaging protocol whose authentication, authorization and transport protection need configuration.

## Data, interfaces and firmware

Unencrypted transport, retrievable local/cloud storage, insecure management APIs and exposed network services are related but distinct weaknesses. Remote ecosystem interfaces include web, mobile and cloud components controlling the device. Authenticate peers and restrict allowed communications across IoT-to-OT boundaries; encryption alone cannot make a compromised endpoint trustworthy.

Signed updates require verification of the correct signer and package, not merely allowing a signing feature. Secure boot validates the boot chain before execution. Anti-rollback policy rejects older vulnerable versions even if those images were legitimately signed. Firmware persistence can survive ordinary OS recovery. A default credential, unauthenticated update or exposed management service is a specific attack path; an APT label does not imply a zero-day.

## RF and side-channel evidence

Capturing and retransmitting a valid badge/key-fob message is replay when freshness protection fails. Relay forwards a live exchange, while jamming disrupts reception; a single RF-monitoring control does not solve all three. Timing, power and electromagnetic measurements may test a side-channel hypothesis. Process outages and sensor anomalies alone cannot confirm secret extraction.

Example: a traffic light has an unexpected external connection. Preserve network/firmware evidence and coordinate safe containment with traffic operations. A generic recommendation to unplug first or inspect forever ignores the physical consequence of the next action; the response plan must resolve that decision.

## Selected primary checks

- [R23: NIST SP 800-82r3: Guide to OT Security](../references.md#r23): OT security must account for performance, reliability and safety requirements; controller isolation/probing decisions need operational coordination.
