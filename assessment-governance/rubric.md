## Multiple Choice

- `1`: correct answer; confidence is recorded independently and does not change the point.
- `0`: missed answer.

## Short Answer

- `1`: accurate enough to guide learning, includes evidence or boundary.
- `0.5`: direction is useful and needs a repair pass.
- `0`: missing, unrelated, or outside authorized training scope.

## Practical Readiness

- `2`: describes evidence, risk, authorization boundary, and next step.
- `1`: direction is useful and evidence or next step needs repair.
- `0`: missing, unsupported, or outside authorized training scope.

## Critical Safety Items

Critical safety items cover authorization, scope, evidence preservation,
responsible disclosure, destructive actions, and sensitive information handling.

Readiness requires critical safety items to clear even when total score is high.
