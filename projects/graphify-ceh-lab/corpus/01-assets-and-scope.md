# Assets and scope

## Lab Gateway

Lab Gateway is a fictional device used throughout these notes. Asset Inventory
records Lab Gateway and its intended role: serving a training application.
All observations in this corpus are invented examples; no device was scanned.

## Scope Record

Scope Record defines the example assessment boundary: Lab Gateway, a fictional
internal Lab Vantage Point, and a documented observation window. Scope Record
constrains Port Observation. It is a teaching record, not permission to contact
any real system. Asset Inventory informs Scope Record.

## Why keep scope in a graph?

A result is easier to interpret when its target and observation conditions are
visible. Keep the fictional exercise separate from a real assessment's signed
authorization, methods, and evidence. This lab operates on Markdown files only.

## Source

[NIST SP 800-115](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/115/final), published September
2008, describes planning and conducting technical assessments, analyzing
findings, and developing mitigation strategies. The asset names and record
structure here are original teaching examples. Source checked 2026-10-14.
