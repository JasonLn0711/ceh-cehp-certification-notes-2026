## What is ready

Version 1.0.0 contains **600 original CEH practice questions**, **two independent 125-item mock forms**, and **25 practical tasks**. Prepared content is not a learner attempt. These are authored educational cases, not official items, recalled exam questions or pass predictions. Independent subject-matter review remains pending.

Stems, choices and hints use professional English. Explanations use plain English. Each question has one intended best answer and three relevant alternatives. Cases use different mechanisms, observations, constraints and decisions; they are not generated by swapping names in one stem. Repeated concepts support retrieval and do not count as independent proof of mastery.

## Run a learning block

1. Confirm the endpoint and remaining shared weekly allowance. Resume unfinished original items and required corrections before opening another set.
2. Teaching mode: show one question, receive choice and low/medium/high confidence, then explain. Drill mode: show ten unanswered items and collect each choice/confidence before releasing feedback at the batch end or endpoint.
3. Score original answers first. Record answered/assigned and correct/answered for partial sets. Keep unattempted items unattempted.
4. Teach every mistake plainly and use at least three materially different retests. A missed retest creates another correction need. Stop at the endpoint and carry named open items. Retests have distinct IDs such as RETEST-<original-ID>-<attempt>-01 and do not inflate the 600.
5. Save actual answers, confidence, version, mode, assistance, retest linkage and actual minutes when known in the [existing attempt workflow](../../assessment-governance/README.md). [Closeout template](../../assessment-governance/daily_closeout_template.md) provides the minimum fields.

The original 600 have IDs CEH26-M01-Q001 through CEH26-M20-Q030, and begin unattempted. Existing historical quiz scores remain unchanged and do not substitute for these IDs. Complete each module's first ten before deeper questions; topics can move with actual instructor delivery.

## Weekly revision and truth boundaries

Review the actual class topics, weak concepts, source changes and coverage at the existing weekly closeout. Read a current primary source for the actual topic or blocker, then revise only where evidence changes the item. Preserve used forms/keys as frozen versions before publishing a revision; never rewrite an old answer against a new key. Update the registry and source date together. Extra trend questions outside the blueprint are optional and do not displace the 600 core IDs. No background job, paid subscription or account activation is created.

A continuous CEH mock requires four hours, already a whole weekly ceiling, and is considered after the first pass. The 25 short tasks do not simulate a six-hour Practical exam. A required first pass needs all 600 first answers with explanations reviewed, all twenty module topic areas studied, and at least one learner-executed accepted task per practical family. Pending retests and formal readiness remain separate.

## Quality check

[Material validation receipt](validation.md) records the performed checks and their limits.

Run `python3 assessments/practice-bank/check.py` from the CEH repo root. It checks counts, unique stems/IDs, keys, registry correspondence, domain allocation and practical fixtures. Structural checks and author review cannot certify psychometric quality or independent expertise. Report an ambiguous item with its ID/version; hold it out of readiness evidence until repaired, retaining the original attempt.

## Closeout examples (synthetic, not learner records)

- A ten-item drill reaches its endpoint after three answers, two correct: record `answered 3/10; correct 2/3; partial`, leave seven unattempted, and preserve the missed item's correction queue. Do not report 2/10 as a completed score.
- An original error is explained and two of its three retests are attempted before time expires: the original score stays unchanged; record the two retest results and the remaining retest. A wrong retest retains its own correction need.
- A hint or key was seen before answering: preserve the response but label it assisted/exposed. It can count as an honestly labeled first encounter, never as an unseen readiness measurement.
- An unattempted item is revised: register its new form version. A previously used form/key is frozen before replacement and old attempts keep their version; a revision does not add another core ID to the 600.
- Loopback service creation is prohibited on the current host: save the precise error as `blocked`; an offline explanation can still be recorded as conceptual work, but family execution remains open.
- The weekly estimate exceeds confirmed capacity: record `at risk`, the remaining questions/corrections/tasks and the proposed revised completion date. Preserve the 240-minute ceiling and ask for the consequential change at that future decision, not for every routine block.
