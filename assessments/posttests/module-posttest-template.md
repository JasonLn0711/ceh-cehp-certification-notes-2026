Use ten original questions: two concepts, three evidence interpretations,
two defensive decisions, two applicable safety judgments and one transfer.
Each has four plausible choices and one defensible best answer. Distribute
correct positions 3/3/2/2 across A–D, varying the sequence between modules.
Tag critical safety in the separate key before administration.

## Administration

Formal mode: closed-book, one question at a time, choice plus `low`, `medium`
or `high` confidence. Withhold feedback and hints until the original set is
scored. Reserve 15 minutes per original set within the agreed learning block;
time expiry records partial progress, with no extension inferred.
Tutoring mode keeps immediate plain-language explanations and does not count
as the formal attempt. Reuse current/next module forms; avoid duplicate generic
quizzes. Never expose the key while administering a formal attempt.

## Scoring and repair

One point per correct original answer, independent of confidence. Record
answered/10 and correct/answered for partial attempts, correct/10 when complete.
Keep categorical confidence by item; leave legacy numeric averages blank.

- `pass`: at least 8/10, all applicable critical-safety items clear, required
  remediation complete, and linked practical acceptance complete.
- `review`: complete at 4–7/10, or at least 8/10 with any safety, remediation
  or practical gate still open.
- `activation_needed`: complete at 0–3/10.
- Partial attempts stay incomplete and cannot receive `pass`.

Every error receives detailed correction and at least three varied retests,
one at a time. Retests have their own score; never add them to the original.
An error on a retest triggers the same rule within the timebox. Keep unfinished
retests open. After a failed score, a fresh versioned ten-item set establishes
an eventual pass; do not re-score the exposed original. A safety failure also
requires a fresh delayed scenario check after correction.

## Evidence claim and closeout

Record question IDs, choices, confidence, original score, safety errors,
linked misconception/retests, practical result and one next action under a dated
attempt. Preserve the rubric/form version in the attempt log. This version
assesses selected-response interpretation and independent practical evidence;
unaided verbal recall is `not assessed`. Old eight-item forms, numerical
confidence and provisional quizzes remain historical and are not converted.

## Acceptance order

without requiring a pre-existing weekly `accepted` label. When conceptual,
remediation, safety and practical components all clear, finalize module passes
and the weekly project acceptance together. Missing components stay open; this
order removes a circular status dependency and waives no evidence requirement.
