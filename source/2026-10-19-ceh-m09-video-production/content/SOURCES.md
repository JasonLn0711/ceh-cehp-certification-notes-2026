## Current-version distinction

RFC9989 was published in May2026 and explicitly obsoletes RFC7489 and9091. The lesson uses its current core semantics; older training references remain historical. Receiver authentication, human identity, business approval and transaction outcome remain different claims. SP800-63B-4 is the July2025 final revision. No unmeasured deployment adoption rate, AI-detector accuracy or assessment-causality claim is made.
